# Wellness & fitness
Scope: fitness, workout tracking, habits, meditation, nutrition, sleep, cycle tracking, coaching. Related: healthcare, social-community, education, media-editorial.
## Users & trust drivers
- Mobile and wearable, daily short sessions; used mid-workout (sweaty hands, glanceable) or in bed (low light, calm).
- Motivation swings: beginners fear judgment; enthusiasts want precise data and progression.
- Trust = credible coaches/science, private health data, forgiving streaks, no body-shaming; destroyed by paywall traps.
## Must-have patterns
- Today view: one primary action (start workout/session), progress rings or simple completion, upcoming plan.
- Active session: huge timer/reps, large tap targets, lock-screen/Live Activity controls, audio cues, pause/skip.
- Logging: quick-add with recents and favorites, barcode scan for food, sensible defaults, edit history.
- Progress: trends over weeks with smoothing, personal records, rest days counted as success.
- Streaks with freezes/grace days; gentle reminders configurable by time and frequency.
- Wearable/health platform sync status (Apple Health, Health Connect) with last-synced time.
## Key screens
- Onboarding goals, today, plan/program, session player, log entry, history, progress, library, profile, subscription, settings/privacy.
## Visual register
- Range: calm and soft (Calm, Oura), high-energy athletic (Nike Training, Strava), data-rich minimal (Whoop, Gentler Streak).
- Dark mode essential for sleep/meditation; large display numerals for timers; motion purposeful, reduce-motion respected.
- Tired default to avoid: neon gradients on black, six-pack stock models, sunset yoga silhouettes, generic leaf/lotus icons.
## Domain rules
- Health data is sensitive: FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, WA My Health My Data Act, GDPR special-category consent.
- Units by locale (kg/lb, km/mi, kcal/kJ) and user-switchable; durations as mm:ss; never mix units in one view.
- Avoid weight/calorie framing by default; offer to hide numbers; no medical claims without evidence; disordered-eating safeguards.
- Subscription: clear trial end date, reminder before charge, cancellation as easy as signup.
## Anti-patterns
- Guilt-trip notifications ("You're letting yourself down"); streak loss as punishment.
- Over-gamification with badges for everything; leaderboards forced on by default.
- Charts with fake perfect upward curves; tiny buttons during active workout.
- Hard paywall after a long onboarding quiz with no preview of value.
