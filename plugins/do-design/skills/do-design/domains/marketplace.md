# Marketplace
Scope: two-sided marketplaces, listings, classifieds, rentals, gig/freelance platforms, booking platforms. Related: ecommerce, travel-hospitality, real-estate, social-community, events-ticketing.
## Users & trust drivers
- Two audiences with different UIs: buyers browse on mobile; sellers/providers manage listings, calendars, payouts often on desktop.
- Trust between strangers is the product: verified identity, reviews, escrow/guarantees, responsive messaging.
- Destroyers: stale listings, fake profiles, off-platform payment requests, total price revealed only at the end.
## Must-have patterns
- Search with map/list toggle, location and date inputs, filters with counts, saved searches and alerts.
- Listing card: primary photo, price with unit (/night, /hr), rating + count, key attributes, favorite heart.
- Listing detail: gallery, host/seller profile block with verification badges, response time, reviews, availability calendar.
- In-app messaging: thread per listing/transaction, read receipts, safety warnings on contact-sharing.
- Booking/checkout: price breakdown, cancellation policy summary, protected payment explanation.
- Seller tools: listing editor with photo guidance, calendar, orders, payouts, performance metrics.
## Key screens
- Home/search, results (map/list), listing detail, profile, messages, checkout/booking, trips/orders, reviews, seller dashboard, listing editor, payouts.
## Visual register
- Range: warm hospitality (Airbnb), utilitarian classifieds (Craigslist-clean, Kleinanzeigen), curated boutique (Etsy, Vinted, Faire).
- User-generated photos dominate: design for inconsistent quality with fixed ratios, object-fit cover, neutral frames.
- Tired default to avoid: generic stock "community" photos, coral-pink Airbnb clones, oversized hero search with empty grid below.
## Domain rules
- Show all-in price early; US FTC junk-fees rule bans drip pricing for short-term lodging and tickets.
- EU DSA: trader identity for business sellers, notice-and-action reporting, explain ranking and paid placement ("Sponsored").
- Reviews: show distribution, dates, and responses; label incentivized reviews; never suppress negative ones.
- Map interactions need list equivalent for keyboard/screen readers; prices on pins need text alternatives.
## Anti-patterns
- Ratings all 4.9-5.0 with round names; sameness of AI-generated listing photos.
- Map pins without clustering; filters that reset on back navigation.
- Hiding fees or cancellation terms behind links; seller dashboard as an afterthought copy of buyer UI.
- Empty-state search ("No results") with no suggestion to widen dates/area.
