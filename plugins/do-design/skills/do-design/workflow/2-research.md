# Phase 2 — Research

Start this right after interview round 1. It runs in the background while the remaining rounds go on.

## Spawn `design-researcher` with
- Brief (2–3 lines), product type, platform, audience, goal.
- Domain card path(s), and the "Research seeds" section pasted in.
- User references (`refs`) and anything to avoid (`avoid`), if known.
- `shots` tool mode from `state.md`, and output paths `<out>/research.md`, `<out>/research/`.
- Skip mode or Quick: 4 exemplars. Standard: 6. Deep: 8 plus competitors.

## When it returns
- Read only its summary of 15 lines or fewer. Open `research.md` only when phase 4 needs detail.
- Write 3–5 takeaways to `state.md` under `## Research`:
  - Convergence: what everyone in the space does. Treat it as the baseline to meet or deliberately beat.
  - Standouts: specific moves by the best products, with the principle behind each.
  - Gaps: opportunities nobody serves well.

## Rules
- Borrow principles, never layouts, branding, copy or assets. The result must not be recognisable as any one product.
- No research possible (offline, no web tools) → rely on the domain card and say so in `state.md`.

Next: `workflow/3-strategy.md`. Wait for the interview to finish; research can still be running.
