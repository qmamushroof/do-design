# E-commerce
Scope: DTC stores, product catalogs, product pages, cart, checkout, retail, subscriptions. Related: marketplace, startup-landing, food-restaurant, media-editorial.
## Users & trust drivers
- Mostly mobile (70%+ traffic), short sessions, browsing on commute; desktop for considered purchases and comparison.
- Trust = real product photography, transparent total cost (shipping, tax, returns) early, genuine reviews, recognizable payment options.
- Destroyers: surprise fees at checkout, forced account creation, popups on landing, out-of-stock after add-to-cart.
## Must-have patterns
- Product card: image with alternate on hover, title, price (sale + original), swatches, rating count; whole card tappable.
- PDP: gallery with zoom, variant pickers showing availability, sticky add-to-cart on mobile, size guide, delivery estimate by location.
- Collection page: filters with result counts, sort, chips for active filters, preserved scroll on back.
- Cart drawer: line items with edit, free-shipping progress, upsell restrained to 1-2 items.
- Checkout: guest by default, express pay (Apple Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal), address autocomplete, order summary always visible.
- Reviews: distribution histogram, filter by rating/fit, verified-buyer labels, customer photos.
## Key screens
- Home, collection/PLP, search results, PDP, cart, checkout, order confirmation, order tracking, account/orders, returns, wishlist.
## Visual register
- Range: editorial luxury (Aesop, SSENSE), playful DTC (Glossier, Liquid Death), utility-dense retail (Uniqlo, Muji).
- Product photography leads; UI recedes. Consistent crop ratio and background across the grid.
- Tired default to avoid: centered hero with "Shop Now" over blurry lifestyle stock, pastel blob backgrounds, countdown timers everywhere.
## Domain rules
- Price display: currency, tax-inclusive per region (EU/UK incl. VAT), EU Omnibus: show lowest 30-day prior price for discounts.
- FTC fake review rule (US): no fabricated/incentivized undisclosed reviews; EU requires stating how reviews are verified.
- EAA (June 2025) makes WCAG 2.1 AA mandatory for EU e-commerce; alt text on all product images.
- Stock/sale status never color-only; low stock claims must be true; easy online cancellation for subscriptions.
- Checkout errors inline, preserve entered data, explain card declines without blaming user.
## Anti-patterns
- Fake urgency ("23 people viewing"), fake scarcity, pre-checked add-ons or insurance.
- Newsletter modal on first paint; chat widget covering mobile add-to-cart.
- Product grids of identical AI-generated items with "$99.99"; star ratings with no review count.
