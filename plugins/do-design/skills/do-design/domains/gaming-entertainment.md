# Gaming & entertainment
Scope: game sites, launchers, stores, esports, streaming, music apps, entertainment, kids' entertainment. Related: social-community, media-editorial, events-ticketing, ecommerce.
## Users & trust drivers
- Players and fans in leisure mode, evenings, on PC, console (10-ft UI), or mobile; high expectations for feel and polish.
- Trust built by fair monetization, honest footage, stable performance, clear patch notes, respect for community.
- Trust destroyed by pay-to-win pressure, obscured currencies, misleading trailers, dark patterns aimed at kids.
## Must-have patterns
- Game page: trailer, real gameplay screenshots, platforms, ESRB/PEGI rating, editions comparison, system requirements.
- Launcher/library: cover-art grid, install/update progress, friends/presence, quick resume.
- Controller + keyboard navigation with visible focus; 10-ft UI with TV safe zones and large type.
- Esports: live scores, brackets, spoiler-free mode, VODs. Music: persistent mini-player, queue, lyrics, offline.
- Kids: parental gate, big-target navigation, read-aloud, no external links or purchases without gate.
## Key screens
- Store/home, title detail, library, launcher, news/patch notes, profile, achievements, friends, esports hub, now playing, settings.
## Visual register
- Each title's art direction leads; platform chrome recessive and dark-friendly.
- Options: cinematic AAA key art, indie hand-made (texture, custom type), Nintendo-like playful clean, esports broadcast graphics.
- Motion and sound are brand; respect reduced-motion and default to muted.
- Avoid tired default: RGB neon on black, glitch text, angular "gamer" HUD frames, purple-cyan gradients.
## Domain rules
- Show age ratings and content descriptors; disclose loot box/random item odds (platform policy, some jurisdictions by law).
- Under-13s: COPPA, UK Age Appropriate Design Code, EU DSA minors rules; privacy by default, no behavioral ads.
- Virtual currency: show real-money equivalent, avoid pack sizes forcing leftovers; clear refunds and cancellation.
- Xbox Accessibility Guidelines: remappable controls, scalable subtitles, colorblind modes; no flashes over 3/second.
## Anti-patterns
- Autoplay trailer with sound on page load.
- Pre-rendered CGI presented as gameplay without labeling.
- Price and editions buried under lore on store pages.
- FOMO timers in kids' products; mouse-only tiny UI in a controller-first launcher.
