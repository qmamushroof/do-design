# Startup & product landing
Scope: marketing sites, product landing pages, waitlists, pricing pages, launch pages, changelogs. Related: saas-dashboard, ai-product, devtools, portfolio-agency.
## Users & trust drivers
- Cold visitors from ads, social, HN; decide in 5-10 s; half or more on mobile; buyers and users ask different questions.
- Trust built by a specific value prop, real product UI, named customers with quotes, transparent pricing, a recent changelog.
- Trust destroyed by buzzwords, unverifiable logos, "Contact sales" for self-serve, dead social links.
## Must-have patterns
- Hero: concrete headline (what + for whom), outcome subline, primary CTA + secondary (demo/docs), real product visual.
- Product proof: annotated screenshots or short looping demos per capability, not abstract icons.
- Social proof: named testimonials with photo/role, case-study metrics, logos only if real and permitted.
- Pricing: 2-4 tiers, monthly/annual toggle with savings explicit, comparison table, FAQ, enterprise path.
- Waitlist: one email field, honest expectations (position, timing), confirmation and double opt-in.
## Key screens
- Home, feature pages, pricing, customers/case studies, changelog, about, waitlist/signup, docs entry, legal.
## Visual register
- Product is the hero; type-driven layouts with strong hierarchy beat illustration.
- Options: editorial long-form (Stripe Press feel), stark monochrome devtool, warm hand-made craft, bold color-blocked consumer.
- One or two committed brand colors used structurally, not gradients on everything.
- Avoid tired default: purple-blue gradient hero, 3 icon feature cards, "Trusted by" grey logo row, glassmorphism fake dashboards.
## Domain rules
- GDPR/ePrivacy: consent banner with equal-weight reject, no non-essential tracking pre-consent; CAN-SPAM/CASL for waitlists.
- FTC endorsement guides and 2024 fake-review rule: genuine testimonials, disclose material connections.
- Pricing clarity: currency, billing period, tax/VAT note, auto-renewal terms, easy cancellation (click-to-cancel laws).
- Core Web Vitals (LCP < 2.5 s, CLS < 0.1); WCAG 2.2 AA; meaningful alt text on product shots.
## Anti-patterns
- "Supercharge your workflow" / "The future of X" headlines that never name what it does.
- Fake dashboard mockups with lorem metrics instead of real UI.
- Animated counters ("10,000+ users") without source; stock 3D blobs and orbs.
- Every tier labeled "Popular"; prices hidden for a self-serve product.
