# Professional services
Scope: law firms, consulting, accounting, architecture/engineering firms, clinic marketing sites, B2B service providers. Related: portfolio-agency, healthcare, startup-landing, fintech.
## Users & trust drivers
- Prospects researching a high-stakes, infrequent decision; desktop at work or mobile after referral; often anxious.
- Trust built by named experts with credentials, specific practice areas, evidenced outcomes, clear fees/process, local presence.
- Trust destroyed by generic stock, vague "solutions", no names or faces, stale insights, buried contact details.
## Must-have patterns
- Service/practice pages: who it's for, problems solved, approach, relevant people, related insights, CTA.
- People directory: filter by practice/office/language; bios with credentials, admissions, publications, contact, vCard.
- Intake: short form with matter type, confidentiality disclaimer, callback window, phone and office map.
- Insights: author, date, topic tags; reports readable without forced gating for basics.
- Consultation booking with fee disclosure; secure client portal entry (documents, invoices, messages).
## Key screens
- Home, services/practice areas, industries, people directory, bio, case studies/results, insights, careers, offices, contact/intake, client portal login.
## Visual register
- Calm, credible, editorial; generous whitespace; serif or refined grotesk headlines, highly legible body.
- Options: editorial journal (McKinsey/Bain insights), boutique warm with real staff photography, modern minimal type-only.
- Restrained palette with one distinctive accent beyond navy; environmental portraits over stock.
- Avoid tired default: navy+gold, gavel/scales/handshake stock, city-skyline hero, "Excellence. Integrity. Results." triad.
## Domain rules
- Law: ABA Model Rules 7.1-7.3 and state rules; no misleading or guaranteed results; "Attorney Advertising" label where required.
- Contact forms state no attorney-client relationship is formed; don't solicit confidential details pre-engagement.
- Clinics: HIPAA-safe intake; no PHI to tracking pixels/analytics. Accountants: CPA/firm licensing stated accurately.
- WCAG 2.2 AA (ADA Title III litigation risk in US); GDPR consent for tracking and newsletters.
## Anti-patterns
- Anonymous testimonials or claims like "We win every case".
- Near-identical SEO-filler practice-area pages.
- Chatbot popping up instantly on a legal or medical inquiry page.
- Grid of identical headshots without bios, credentials, or contact.
