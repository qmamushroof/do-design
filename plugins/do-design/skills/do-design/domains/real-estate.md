# Real estate
Scope: property listings, rentals, mortgages, brokerage/agent sites, property management, tenant portals. Related: marketplace, fintech, travel-hospitality, professional-services.
## Users & trust drivers
- Buyers/renters search intensively for weeks on mobile and desktop; high stakes, emotional, often co-deciding.
- Trust built by accurate photos/floor plans, price history, days on market, transparent fees, verified agents.
- Trust destroyed by stale or fake listings, bait pricing, lead forms gating basics, hidden broker fees.
## Must-have patterns
- Search: map with draw-area, list/map split, saved searches with alerts, filters (price, beds, baths, type, pets, commute).
- Listing card: photo, price, beds/baths/area, status badge (new, pending, reduced); favorites/compare shareable with co-buyers.
- Detail: gallery grid + floor plan + virtual tour, key-facts table, price/tax history, neighborhood, schools, commute.
- Affordability/mortgage calculator: editable assumptions, payment split (principal/interest, tax, insurance, HOA).
- Tenant/PM portal: rent payment, maintenance requests with photos and status, lease docs, notices.
## Key screens
- Search map/list, listing detail, saved homes, compare, tour booking, mortgage calculator, pre-qualification, agent profile, tenant dashboard, maintenance request.
## Visual register
- Photo-led with neutral chrome; facts in clean tabular layout; map as first-class surface.
- Options: luxury editorial (serif, wide margins, slow pacing), dense utility portal (Zillow/Rightmove), warm local-neighborhood.
- Neutral base, status colors for availability, restrained accent for CTAs.
- Avoid tired default: drone shot of a glass villa hero, navy+gold "luxury" palette, keys/handshake stock imagery.
## Domain rules
- US Fair Housing Act: no discriminatory wording or targeting (e.g. "ideal for singles"); Equal Housing Opportunity logo.
- Mortgage ads (TILA/Reg Z): a stated rate requires APR; triggering terms require full disclosures; show NMLS IDs.
- Area units and basis explicit (sq ft vs m², gross vs net); currency; listing date and last-updated shown.
- Broker/application fees upfront where required (NYC FARE Act 2025); MLS/IDX attribution; WCAG 2.2 AA with list alternative to map.
## Anti-patterns
- Requiring sign-up or phone number to see photos or price history.
- Unclustered map pins; filters that reset when the map pans.
- Calculators with hidden default rates and no tax/insurance.
- Wide-angle-distorted galleries with no floor plan or room labels.
