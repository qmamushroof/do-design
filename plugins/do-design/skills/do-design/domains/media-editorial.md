# Media & editorial
Scope: news, magazines, blogs, newsletters, publishing, podcasts, video/streaming catalogs. Related: social-community, ecommerce, education, portfolio-agency, gaming-entertainment.
## Users & trust drivers
- Readers arrive from search/social links on mobile, read once; loyal subscribers return daily; viewers lean back on TV.
- Trust = clear bylines, dates, corrections, sourcing, labeled opinion vs news vs sponsored.
- Destroyers: ad clutter, layout shift, autoplay video, paywall bait-and-switch, clickbait headlines.
## Must-have patterns
- Article page: headline, dek, byline with author page, published/updated timestamps, reading time, share, related.
- Typographic body: 60-75ch measure, 17-21px, real pull quotes, captions, credits, footnotes/links styled clearly.
- Home/section front: hierarchy via size and position, not color; breaking/live labels with timestamps.
- Live blog: reverse-chronological updates, pinned summary, timestamps, new-updates indicator not auto-jumping.
- Audio/video player: persistent mini-player, chapters, speed, transcript, resume position.
- Paywall/meter: honest count, clear price, cancel terms; newsletter signup inline not modal.
## Key screens
- Front page, section, article, live blog, author, topic/tag, search, newsletter archive, podcast episode, show/title detail, subscribe, account.
## Visual register
- Range: classic serif authority (NYT, FT), bold modern editorial (The Verge, Bloomberg Businessweek), quiet literary (The Browser, Aeon).
- Typography is the brand: distinctive display face plus readable text face; color used sparingly as section coding.
- Imagery: real photojournalism with credits; streaming uses large key art with consistent ratios.
- Tired default to avoid: card grid of identical rounded thumbnails, stock "newspaper and coffee" images, generic sans everything.
## Domain rules
- Label sponsored/advertorial content clearly (FTC endorsement guides, EU DSA ad transparency).
- GDPR/ePrivacy consent: reject as easy as accept; no pre-ticked boxes; pay-or-consent needs real choice.
- Dates absolute on articles ("Nov 25, 2026, 9:40 a.m. ET") plus "Updated" with what changed; corrections appended.
- Captions/transcripts for audio/video (WCAG 2.2 AA, EAA for EU e-books/AV services); no autoplay with sound.
## Anti-patterns
- Lorem headlines like "Breaking: Major Event Shakes World"; every card the same size.
- Cumulative layout shift from late ads; sticky video covering text on mobile.
- Hero carousels on news fronts; tiny low-contrast gray body text in light weights.
