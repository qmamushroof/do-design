# Food & restaurant
Scope: restaurant sites, online ordering, delivery apps, recipes, grocery, cafés, meal kits. Related: ecommerce, marketplace, logistics-industrial, wellness-fitness.
## Users & trust drivers
- Hungry, impatient, mobile-first, often one-handed; repeat orders dominate; diners want hours, menu, booking fast.
- Trust built by accurate ETAs, live tracking, real dish photos, clear fees, reliable allergen info.
- Trust destroyed by PDF menus, wrong hours, surprise service/delivery fees, missing items with no refund path.
## Must-have patterns
- Menu: sticky category nav, item rows with price, dietary tags (V, VG, GF), modifier sheet with required/optional groups.
- Cart: persistent mini-cart, itemized fees, delivery/pickup toggle, time-slot picker, one-tap reorder.
- Tracking: status stepper with live map, courier contact, ETA updates, substitution approval for grocery.
- Restaurant site: hours/address/phone above fold, reservation widget, click-to-call, maps link, HTML menu.
- Recipe: servings scaler, unit toggle, step-by-step cook mode with large type and screen wake lock, jump-to-recipe.
## Key screens
- Discovery home, restaurant/store page, menu, item modifier sheet, cart, checkout, tracking, order history, reservations, recipe detail, account.
## Visual register
- Photography carries the brand: consistent lighting, real food, no stock; text over food needs scrims.
- Options: warm editorial (serif, cream paper), bold graphic street-food (heavy grotesk, flat color), clean grocery utility.
- Appetizing warms or one strong brand hue; never tint food photos with color overlays.
- Avoid tired default: red/yellow fast-food palette, overhead flat-lay hero, chalkboard textures, script "Delicious" headings.
## Domain rules
- Allergens: EU/UK 14 regulated allergens available before ordering; US 9 major; distinguish "contains" vs "may contain"; never color-only.
- US chains 20+ locations: calories on menus incl. online; service/delivery fees shown before checkout (FTC/state junk-fee laws).
- Alcohol: age confirmation at checkout, ID check on delivery, local licensing hours.
- Out-of-stock and substitutions explicit; failed payment preserves cart; WCAG 2.2 AA, 44px+ tap targets.
## Anti-patterns
- Menu as PDF or image with no selectable text, prices, or dietary info.
- Autoplay video hero hiding hours and address.
- Recipe life story before ingredients with no jump link.
- Modifier sheets that reveal required choices only when "Add to cart" fails.
