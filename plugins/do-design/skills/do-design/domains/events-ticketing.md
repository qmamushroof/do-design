# Events & ticketing
Scope: events, conferences, concerts, ticketing, booking, venues, festivals, meetups. Related: travel-hospitality, marketplace, ecommerce, gaming-entertainment.
## Users & trust drivers
- Attendees buy on mobile, often during high-demand on-sales under time pressure; organizers manage on desktop.
- Trust built by all-in pricing, clear seat views, secure transfer/resale, mobile tickets that work at the door.
- Trust destroyed by drip fees, queue crashes, bots/scalpers, unclear refunds, tickets not loading at entry.
## Must-have patterns
- Event page: date/time with timezone, venue + map, lineup/agenda, price range, availability, add-to-calendar, share.
- Seat map: zoomable, section-to-seat drill-down, price legend, view-from-seat, accessible list alternative, not color-only.
- Ticket selection: tiers with inclusions, quantity limits, visible hold timer, all-in price by default.
- Virtual queue: honest position/ETA, "don't refresh" guidance, place retained across disconnects.
- Tickets: Apple/Google Wallet, offline QR, transfer/resale, accessible seating requests.
## Key screens
- Discover, event detail, agenda/schedule, seat map, ticket selection, queue, checkout, confirmation, my tickets, transfer, organizer dashboard, check-in.
## Visual register
- Event identity drives the page: poster art, bold display type, strong color fields per event.
- Options: gig-poster expressive (DICE), utilitarian conference agenda, premium venue editorial, festival maximalist.
- Platform chrome stays neutral so varied artwork works; dark mode suits nightlife.
- Avoid tired default: crowd-hands-in-stage-lights hero, confetti, generic purple neon gradient.
## Domain rules
- FTC junk-fee rule (May 2025): total price incl. mandatory fees shown first and most prominently; similar UK/EU rules.
- Refund, transfer, and resale-cap policies before purchase; BOTS Act; per-order limits.
- ADA: accessible seating bookable online on equal terms; WCAG 2.2 AA; timers extendable (WCAG 2.2.1).
- Times in venue timezone; doors vs start distinguished; age restrictions displayed.
## Anti-patterns
- Fees revealed only at the last step; unexplained "service fee".
- Agenda as a static image; sessions without time, room, or add-to-calendar.
- Seat maps with tiny tap targets and no list view.
- Fake "selling fast" badges on every event.
