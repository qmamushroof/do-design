# HR & recruiting
Scope: job boards, career sites, ATS, HRIS, payroll, performance, employee self-service portals. Related: crm-enterprise, saas-dashboard, fintech, education.
## Users & trust drivers
- Candidates: mobile, anxious, comparing many jobs. Recruiters: desktop power users at volume. Employees: infrequent self-service.
- Trust built by salary transparency, clear process/timeline, status updates, accurate pay, private personal records.
- Trust destroyed by ghosting, retyping a CV after upload, ghost jobs, payroll errors, exposed salary/SSN data.
## Must-have patterns
- Job search: keyword + location + remote filter, salary range on cards, posted date, saved jobs/alerts.
- Job detail: summary, pay range, location/remote policy, benefits, hiring steps, sticky apply CTA on mobile.
- Application: CV parse with editable prefill, under 5 minutes, save progress, post-submit status tracker.
- ATS pipeline: kanban by stage, bulk move/reject with templated messages, scorecards, interview scheduling.
- HRIS: employee profile, time-off balance/request, payslips, documents, org chart, approvals inbox.
## Key screens
- Job search, job detail, application, candidate dashboard, career site, ATS pipeline, candidate profile, scorecard, scheduling, employee home, payslip, time-off, org chart, onboarding checklist.
## Visual register
- Candidate-facing: warm, human, real-team employer-brand photography. Recruiter/HRIS: calm, dense, neutral.
- Options: friendly consumer (Welcome to the Jungle), crisp enterprise (Rippling, Workday), editorial employer-brand career site.
- Neutral core with one accent; status chips always labeled; no confetti in payroll.
- Avoid tired default: diverse-stock-team high-five hero, rocket/ladder illustrations, purple HR-tech gradients.
## Domain rules
- Pay transparency (CO, CA, NY, WA, IL; EU Pay Transparency Directive, June 2026): salary ranges in postings.
- EEO/OFCCP self-ID voluntary, separate from application, hidden from reviewers; inclusive job language.
- Automated screening: bias audit and candidate notice (NYC Local Law 144, EU AI Act high-risk); human review path.
- GDPR/CCPA retention and talent-pool consent; mask SSN/bank details; WCAG 2.2 AA with accommodation request path.
## Anti-patterns
- Account creation required before viewing or applying.
- "Competitive salary" with no range; fake urgency ("50 people applied").
- Status stuck on "In review" forever; no rejection notice.
- Personality-quiz gimmicks and gamified badges in serious HR flows.
