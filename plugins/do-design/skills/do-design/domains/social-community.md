# Social & community
Scope: social networks, forums, communities, group chat/messaging, dating, creator platforms. Related: media-editorial, marketplace, gaming-entertainment, events-ticketing, productivity.
## Users & trust drivers
- Mobile-dominant, high-frequency, habitual; moderators and community managers on desktop with heavy queues.
- Trust = feeling safe (block, mute, report work), control over visibility, authentic people, transparent ranking.
- Destroyers: harassment without recourse, bots/fakes, manipulative engagement loops, leaked private info.
## Must-have patterns
- Feed item: author with identity cues (name, handle, verification), timestamp, content, actions (reply, react, share, more).
- Composer: drafts, media attach, audience/visibility selector, character count, alt-text prompt for images.
- Threads: nested or flat with clear reply context; collapse long threads; "new since last visit" marker.
- Messaging: delivery/read states, typing, reactions, reply-quote, message requests from strangers filtered.
- Safety: block/mute/report within 2 taps, content warnings, keyword filters, privacy settings in plain language.
- Notifications grouped and filterable (mentions vs likes); granular controls.
## Key screens
- Feed, post detail/thread, profile, compose, search/explore, community/group home, messages, notifications, settings/privacy, moderation queue.
## Visual register
- Range: content-first neutral (Threads, Bluesky), cozy community (Discord, Geneva), editorial forum (Are.na, Hacker News-clean), expressive dating (Hinge).
- UI chrome recedes behind user content; avatars and names carry identity; dense timestamps and counts in muted text.
- Tired default to avoid: diverse-hands-in-circle stock photos, speech-bubble logos, gradient avatars with fake heart counts.
## Domain rules
- EU DSA and UK Online Safety Act: reporting flows, appeal of moderation decisions, age assurance where required.
- COPPA/age gates for under-13; minors default to private; no public location for minors.
- Alt text prompts, captions for video/audio; reactions and status labeled for screen readers, not emoji-only.
- Counts abbreviated (1.2K) with exact on hover; relative timestamps with absolute on hover.
## Anti-patterns
- Infinite scroll with no stopping cues; manipulative "you have 99+ notifications" red badges.
- Feeds of identical AI faces and generic captions; every post with 1.2K likes.
- Report buried 3 menus deep; block that notifies the blocked user.
- Dating UIs reducing people to swipes with no context; dark-pattern premium prompts.
