# Government & nonprofit
Scope: government services, civic tech, public information, charities, NGOs, donations, volunteering. Related: healthcare, education, professional-services, events-ticketing.
## Users & trust drivers
- Everyone: low literacy, low bandwidth, older, disabled, non-native speakers; often stressed, mostly mobile.
- Trust built by official identity (domain, crest, banner), plain language, predictable steps, clear fees/timelines, receipts.
- Donors trust impact evidence, financial transparency, secure checkout, easy recurring-gift management.
## Must-have patterns
- Start page: what it's for, eligibility, cost, time to complete, what you need, single "Start now".
- One-thing-per-page forms, check-your-answers summary, confirmation with reference number and next steps.
- Error summary linking to fields plus inline messages; save-and-return; timeout warning with extend; never discard input.
- Donation form: preset amounts with impact labels, one-off/monthly toggle, Gift Aid/tax receipt, cover-fees opt-in, wallet pay.
- Language switcher, phone/in-person fallback, official site banner, status tracking for applications.
## Key screens
- Service start page, eligibility checker, multi-step form, check answers, confirmation, status tracker, guidance page, donate, campaign, impact report, volunteer signup.
## Visual register
- Clarity over brand: highly legible type (Public Sans, GDS Transport, Source Sans 3), 16-19px base, strong focus states.
- Gov: neutral palette, minimal imagery. Nonprofit: warmer, documentary photography that preserves dignity.
- Options: GOV.UK austere utility, USWDS civic modern, campaign-bold nonprofit (charity: water), annual-report editorial.
- Avoid tired default: hands-holding-heart icons, poverty-porn imagery, teal-green eco gradients, mission-statement carousel.
## Domain rules
- WCAG 2.2 AA minimum; Section 508 (US), EN 301 549/European Accessibility Act (EU), UK PSBAR accessibility statement.
- Reuse GOV.UK Design System or USWDS patterns; US 21st Century IDEA: accessible, mobile-friendly, consistent federal sites.
- Plain language (reading age ~9-12), no unexplained acronyms; unambiguous dates and money.
- Data minimisation, clear privacy notice, no dark patterns; show charity registration number and tax-deductibility.
## Anti-patterns
- Custom form controls that break assistive tech.
- PDF-only forms or key info buried in downloads.
- Pre-checked recurring donations or monthly as hidden default.
- Hero carousels and stock photos of officials instead of the task.
