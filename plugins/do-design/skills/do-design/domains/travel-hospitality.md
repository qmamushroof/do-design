# Travel & hospitality
Scope: flights, hotels, vacation rentals, car hire, trip planning, tours, tourism boards. Related: marketplace, ecommerce, events-ticketing, food-restaurant.
## Users & trust drivers
- Planners browse leisurely on desktop/tablet; travelers act under stress on mobile, often offline or roaming.
- Trust built by total price upfront, clear cancellation terms, real photos, verified reviews, instant confirmation.
- Trust destroyed by fees at checkout, fake urgency, ambiguous dates/timezones, unreachable support.
## Must-have patterns
- Search: destination autocomplete, date-range picker with flexible dates/price calendar, guest/room selector, recent searches.
- Results: map/list toggle with synced hover, sort by total price, filters (price, free cancellation, amenities, stops).
- Detail: tagged photo grid, amenities, policies, map, category reviews; fare/rate table (bags, changes, refunds) included vs excluded.
- Seat/room map: zoomable, accessible list alternative, price legend, unavailable not shown by color alone.
- Trips wallet: confirmation codes, offline access, add-to-calendar, check-in, gate/status push updates.
## Key screens
- Search home, results list/map, listing/flight detail, fare selection, seat map, traveler details, checkout, confirmation, trips/itinerary, manage booking, support.
## Visual register
- Imagery-led on inspiration pages; dense scannable cards on results; utility first in booking flow.
- Options: editorial magazine (serif headlines, full-bleed photography), utilitarian fare grid (Google Flights), warm boutique hospitality.
- Neutral UI with one confident accent; deal colors sparing; tourism boards may draw on local cultural palettes.
- Avoid tired default: beach-sunset hero with centered search pill, teal/orange "wanderlust" palette, stock couples in hammocks.
## Domain rules
- US DOT full-fare rule: advertised airfare includes mandatory taxes/fees; FTC junk-fee rule: total lodging price shown first.
- EU Package Travel Directive and Omnibus price rules; cancellation deadlines shown in property-local time.
- Times always with timezone, +1 day arrival markers, layover duration, airport codes with city names.
- Passport-name matching guidance, unambiguous DOB format; accessibility filters (accessible rooms, assistance requests); WCAG 2.2 AA; ADA lodging accessibility info on detail pages (US).
## Anti-patterns
- "Only 1 room left!" countdowns and scarcity badges on every card.
- Large per-night price with taxes/cleaning fees hidden until final step.
- Carousel-only gallery with no grid; map that cannot be dismissed on mobile.
- Date pickers that hide the second month or block typed entry.
